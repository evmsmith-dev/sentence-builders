    ℹ️ About Sentence Builders

    A grammar, punctuation and sentence game for grades 3–8. Fix and build sentences, and every level you pass puts up a building on Grammar Island.

    How to play

    • Pick a world, then a skill and a level. Each level starts with a Tip Card that explains the rule.
    • Right on the first try is 1 point; on the second try, ½. Pass a level with 6 of 8 points; 7 earns two stars and 8 earns three. (Shorter levels scale the same way.)
    • Levels for your grade and below are open from the start. Pass a level to open the next one.
    • Each skill has a badge: Bronze for passing level 1, Silver for level 2, and Gold for scoring 7 or more on level 3 on two different days. More badges come from finishing worlds and from steady practice.
    • Forgot a rule? Open 📘 Rule Book, or tap 📘 Labels during a Q&A Flow.

    What it's based on

    Minnesota standards. The skills follow the 2020 Minnesota K–12 Academic Standards in English Language Arts. Each skill lists the benchmarks it practices (for example, 4.2.1.1 is grade 4 punctuation, spelling, capitalization and grammar). The Tip Cards also use the words teachers and tests often use, like parenthetical and nonrestrictive.

    Shurley English. Flow Factory follows the Shurley English method many schools teach: the Question and Answer Flow, its word labels (SN, V, Adj and the rest, including compound parts like CSN and CDO), the seven sentence patterns, the Skill Check mark-up (noun check, parentheses around prepositional phrases, a slash before the verb, the complete subject underlined once and the complete predicate twice) and sentence diagramming. Each Shurley level is assumed to match the player's grade.

    Grades. Grades 3–5 get Punctuation Port, Word Works (including word parts and word choice), Sentence Studio (including figurative language, revising and typed sentence combining), Punctuation Port (including Listen and Write) and Flow Factory (including compound labels and parts of speech). Sentence Diagramming opens in grade 5. Grade 6 adds Revision Ridge and Advanced Q&A Flow for review and grades 6–8 skills.

    Sentence Builders is a practice supplement for skills taught in class. Its sentences, hints, Tip Cards and drawings are original: it uses no text, examples or jingles from Shurley English or other curricula, and it isn't made by or connected to Shurley Instructional Materials or the Minnesota Department of Education.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no ads, and nothing is sent anywhere. Progress stays on this device; use Settings → Backup to move it to another one. After the first visit it works offline.
